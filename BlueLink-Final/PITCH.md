# Sunday demo — What should this company know this week?
Based on the 13 November Blue Virtual SP Hackathon presentation, pages 2–10, and the written Student Challenge.

**Problem:** A small 10–25 person company is busy building. It misses a testbed, a funding call or the right partner because nobody has time to follow scattered sources.

**Solution:** BlueLink is a regional innovation radar. Understand members → scan and deduplicate public information → match and explain → present actionable findings and learn from feedback. Start with the official member directory; build an evidence-led public profile and allow a company to refine its own priorities. Local physical events and partners are prioritized when relevant; webinars are not geographically penalized.

## Demo sequence
1. Staff signs in and shows the official member directory link and the three seeded members. Explain that directory discovery is available for future expansion but do not run it during the three-company demo. Do not claim verified full membership. Show three company profiles, at least one successful live website extraction, source evidence and inferred interests.
2. Provision separate company logins. Sign in as a member. Show the five-item weekly brief and a concrete next action. Compare two company rankings. Edit a priority and demonstrate recalculation.
3. Open funding/testing results from at least two sources (Vinnova and RISE). Show Ymner as supplementary discovery, verify an original call URL via authorized import. Do not represent a closed or unverified call as open.
4. Demonstrate a local event/partner preference and an online webinar with no geographic penalty. If a real event is unavailable, explicitly label the example simulated and explain the ranking rule.
5. Ask a relevant question, unrelated question and instruction override/private-data request. Show human referral for formal eligibility. Show staff-only public insights and company account isolation.
6. Explain test evidence and limitations. Handover source, README, DEPLOY and EVALUATION. Discuss full directory coverage, centralized monitoring, real provider testing and production identity as next steps.

**Value:** Less search effort, understandable evidence and useful next actions for small Blekinge companies. Time savings are a hypothesis, not a measured claim. The final slide's question is the product goal: what should this company know this week?
