# BlueLink AI — demo pitch

## Problem
Companies waste time searching fragmented research, funding, testing, events and potential collaborators. A useful Virtual Science Park should understand a company before asking it to fill in a long questionnaire.

## Solution
BlueLink builds evidence-led public profiles, lets companies refine their priorities and ranks public findings with transparent explanations. A bounded assistant and staff view support innovation rather than unrelated tasks.

## Suggested demo sequence (adapt to organizer's pitch duration)
1. Sign in, select Cleura, show facts vs inferred interests and original evidence.
2. Refine priorities; compare ranked funding/testing findings with DNV Cyber or AFRY.
3. Show research/news/events from a successful live refresh; if unavailable, explicitly enable labelled simulated examples instead.
4. Show partner suggestions based on common or complementary public capabilities; no confirmed collaboration intent.
5. Show save/feedback, profile-change history and alert preferences. Show staff public insights with a separately prepared staff account.
6. Ask a supported question, an unrelated question and an override/private-data request. Demonstrate safe redirection and human referral.
7. Present tests, source diagnostics, real verification limitations and the plan to scale beyond three companies.

## Value
Less discovery effort; relevant and explainable findings; evidence for decisions; potential introductions; public ecosystem insights for Blue Science Park staff. Treat time savings as a hypothesis to validate with company users, not a measured result.

## Next steps
Verified member onboarding, more source adapters and coverage, centralized public retrieval cache, human relevance validation, secure production authentication, managed scheduling and tested email delivery.
